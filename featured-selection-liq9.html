Whisky Guide

What's the difference between Scotch, Bourbon and Japanese whisky?+
  • Scotch — made in Scotland, aged min. 3 years. Ranges from Speyside fruit to Islay smoke.
  • Bourbon — American, min. 51% corn, new charred oak. Sweet vanilla and caramel.
  • Japanese — follows Scottish methods with Japanese precision. Smooth, complex, globally awarded.
I'm new to whisky — where should I start?+

Start with Johnnie Walker Black (฿780). For something bolder try Maker's Mark Bourbon. For single malt, Tamnavulin Double Cask from Speyside is approachable. Avoid heavily peated Islay until you've developed your palate.

Cognac Guide

What's the difference between VS, VSOP and XO?+
  • VS — min. 2 years aged. Fruity, accessible. Best in cocktails.
  • VSOP — min. 4 years. More complex. Good neat or on ice.
  • XO — min. 10 years. Rich, deep, luxurious. The prestige gift tier.
Why is Cognac so popular in Thailand?+

Cognac penetration in Thailand is nearly 3x the global average. It's deeply embedded in Thai gifting culture — the bottle communicates respect and celebration across all social occasions.
