Whisky Guide

Scotch vs Bourbon vs Japanese?

Scotch — Scotland, min 3yr aged. Speyside fruit to Islay smoke.

Bourbon — American, 51%+ corn, new charred oak. Vanilla and caramel.

Japanese — Scottish methods + Japanese precision. Smooth and complex.

New to whisky — where to start?

Start with Johnnie Walker Black (฿780). For bourbon try Maker's Mark. For single malt, Tamnavulin Double Cask from Speyside is approachable.
