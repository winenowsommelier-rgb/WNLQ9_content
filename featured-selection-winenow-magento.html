Wine Buying Guide

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, gifting and choosing the right wine in Thailand.

Choosing the Right Wine

I'm new to wine — where should I start?

Start with fruit-forward styles. For red wine try Malbec from Argentina (฿380-฿600). For white wine, Sauvignon Blanc from Marlborough is immediately enjoyable.

How do I pair wine with Thai food?

Sauvignon Blanc or off-dry Riesling — green curry, som tam

Pinot Noir or Grenache rose — grilled meats, larb, duck

Viognier or Marsanne — coconut-based dishes, massaman curry

Champagne or sparkling wine — aperitifs and seafood starters

Avoid heavy tannic reds with very spicy dishes.

Storage & Collecting

How should I store wine at home in Thailand?

Ideal storage is 12-16 degrees Celsius with humidity around 60-70%. Store bottles in the coolest, darkest part of your home. Wine-Now stocks Temptech and Dometic dual-zone wine fridges.

Does Wine-Now carry investment-grade wines?

Yes - we carry Bordeaux First Growths, Burgundy Grand Cru (DRC, Rousseau, Leflaive), Penfolds Grange, Petrus and prestige Champagne. We also offer Bordeaux en primeur.
