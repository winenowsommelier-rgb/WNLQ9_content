## 2️⃣ PROCESS OVERVIEW & DEFINITION

### What This Process Is
**"Premium Content Replacement Workflow"** — A systematic, multi-phase process for:
1. Identifying underperforming/review-status content
2. Discovering market trends & competitive opportunities
3. Creating premium-aligned replacement topics with full research integration
4. Preparing production-ready briefs in a consistent template format
5. Updating content management systems with new items

### Process Name & Classification
- **Category:** Content Strategy & Production
- **Type:** Replacement + Upgrade (not enhancement)
- **Scope:** Multi-topic, multi-site, data-driven
- **Output:** Production-ready content briefs in standardized template

### Core Workflow Phases
**PHASE 1: DISCOVERY & ALIGNMENT** (30 min) — Clarify replace (not enhance) intent; identify old content; confirm premium positioning (Collector, Enthusiast, Explorer personas); define success metrics.

**PHASE 2: MARKET RESEARCH** (45 min) — Web search trending topics; extract growth rates, market data, producer info; identify emerging regions; compile specific stories.

**PHASE 3: TOPIC IDEATION & SELECTION** (30 min) — Organize findings; select 10 topics balanced across sites (5 WN, 5 LIQ9), focus areas, and growth potential; create selection summary.

**PHASE 4: COMPLETE BRIEF CREATION** (60 min) — For each topic develop Content Brief (KEY/TENSION/STORY), Image Strategy, Controversy Angle, Production Notes, FAQ, Persona targeting.

**PHASE 5: RESEARCH INTEGRATION** (45 min) — Weave specific producer names, exact market data/growth rates, auction records, price points; ensure 3-5 research data points per brief.

**PHASE 6: DATABASE REPLACEMENT** (30 min) — Fetch Notion schema; create new pages; populate fields; map new→old; set Status = "Not started".

**PHASE 7: HANDOFF & DOCUMENTATION** (20 min) — Summarize topics; document remaining steps; prepare memory document + quick-start guide.

**Total Process Time:** ~4.5 hours (or 1 focused session)

## 8️⃣ ADJUSTMENTS FOR FUTURE ITERATIONS

### What Worked Well
1. Phased approach (7 phases made large task manageable)
2. Research integration (specific producer names > generic angles)
3. Template reference (May 2026 database as structure guide)
4. Mapping document (clear 1:1 replacement documentation)
5. Research-first (gather market data BEFORE ideating topics)

### What to Improve Next Time
1. **Image prompts earlier** — create during Phase 4, not later
2. **Writer handoff package** — one-pager per topic (word count, research points, CTA placement, FAQ)
3. **Video script drafts** — 30-60 sec angle per topic for social
4. **Competitor comparison** — add "why this beats competitor X" to Controversy
5. **Regional customization** — note Thai cultural nuances during Phase 5 (not at translation)
6. **Close the loop on deletion** — explicitly confirm old items are archived/deleted, don't leave both sets live

### Future Process Enhancements
1. **Market research templates** — reusable queries ("Premium market trends 2026", "Investment opportunities emerging regions", "Luxury consumer behavior collector psychology", "Craft/artisanal production innovation")
2. **Topic selection scoring rubric** — market growth (CAGR %), collector interest, search volume, social buzz, e-commerce tie-in
3. **Template expansion** — add fields: Anchor statistic, Collector quote, Controversy quote, Social media angles, Video script outline
4. **Bilingual planning** — note Thai cultural relevance, regional producer ties, language nuances during Phase 4

## 9️⃣ OPEN-SOURCE PROCESS COMPARISON

| Framework | Source | Advantage | Why ours differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content Audit + Replacement | Agencies/publishers | Structured, audit-first | Ours is research-first → faster decisions |
| Pillar + Cluster | HubSpot, Semrush | SEO-optimized linking | Ours works for replacement (maps old→new, keeps traffic) |
| Agile Content Sprint | Netflix/SaaS | Parallel, cross-functional | Ours is solo-friendly, single session |
| Content Operations | Contently, Skyword | Enterprise governance | Ours is lightweight, manual approval per phase |

**Recommended hybrid:** Add **Pillar-Cluster** structure during Phase 3. For each Pillar
(comprehensive, 1500-2000 words) identify 2-3 related Cluster posts (500-800 words) and an
internal-linking plan. Example for Item #1 (Portfolio Strategy pillar): clusters on "Burgundy
Allocation 41.3%", "Younger Vintages Trend", "Liv-ex Index Tracking". Benefits: SEO authority,
long-tail traffic, distributed writing load.
