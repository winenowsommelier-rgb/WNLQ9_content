# 04 — The Monthly Content Engine (repeatable workflow)

> Mirror of §15 of the Notion *Editorial Production Standard v2*. This is how we build a whole
> month from blank calendar to handoff. Run steps in order. Never skip Step 1 (research) or the two
> council passes (Steps 3 and 5). Proven on June 2026 (~86 rows) and July 2026 (80 rows).

## Step 0 — Lock the month's parameters
Confirm with the owner before any topic work:
- **Volume & cadence** — Full engine (~80, 2–3/day) · Lean (~40) · Mid (~55–60).
- **Evidence basis** — validate with live keyword/SERP/trend data, or editorial judgment.
- **Focus anchor** — a campaign/sale window, a seasonal theme, or a balanced month.

## Step 1 — Live-signal research (always before topic design)
Pull and record, tagging factual specifics `[VERIFY]`:
- **Drinks calendar** — international/national drink days (WSET 2026 calendar, Master of Malt).
- **Thai seasonal & cultural context** — rainy season, festivals, gifting moments.
- **Thai alcohol SALES-BAN dates (hard compliance pin)** — the 5 Buddhist holy days: Makha
  Bucha, Visakha Bucha, Asalha Bucha, Khao Phansa (start of Lent), Ok Phansa. **They shift each
  year — confirm exact dates every month** and pin them to the calendar first.
- **E-commerce windows** — 7.7, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12 (handle per the naming rule in §15.6 / below).
- Pin timely pieces first; evergreen fills around them.

## Step 2 — Cluster architecture (hub-and-spoke)
- Design **6–8 topic clusters** for the month: each = **1 Pillar hub + 4–8 Blog/Social spokes**.
- Anchor each cluster to a date (timely) or evergreen need.
- **Cross-month linking:** new hubs link back to prior-month pillars; spokes link up to their hub.
- **Balance:** ~50/50 WN/LIQ9 · ~2–3 posts/day · never two similar pieces adjacent · weekends lighter.
- **Priority tiers:** Hero ~15% · Standard ~55% · Filler ~30%.

## Step 3 — Council pre-flight (review the PLAN, before writing)
Run the §12 council on the **plan** (titles, angles, dates, cluster map). Catch compliance, dedupe,
and voice issues at the cheapest stage. Get owner sign-off on the calendar + any swaps.

## Step 4 — Build the month
- New month = a **new sibling database** `YYYY MON — WNLQ9 — Content Production` under the
  **2026 Content Calendar Hub**, **mirroring the schema exactly**; add that month's **Month** option.
- Create rows in **batches of 6–8** with full metadata + Content Brief + KEY/STORY/TENSION +
  bilingual **DRAFT v1**, Status = **Review**.
- **DRAFT v1 = direct-answer skeleton** (direct answer 40–60w → section build → FAQ → CTA, TH+EN).
  It is *not* the final word-count article — Hero expansion is a separate step (Step 6).

## Compliance rules that bite every month (§15.6)
- **Never name/sell a sale.** No "7.7/11.11" or *buy/cart/shopping list/stock up/% off* in titles,
  body or social. Reframe as **decision-stage education** ("How to choose…", "What every home bar
  needs"), timed to the window, price-on-request / LINE CTA. Complies *and* ranks better.
- **Sales-ban days = secular + non-commercial.** No product, no price, no "buy," **and no reference
  to Buddhism or the religious reason.** Reframe to mindful / non-alcoholic / wellness.
- **Magento = no emoji.** Published Title + Content TH/EN are **plain text only** ("-" bullets).

## Step 5 — Council QA + live verification
- Run the full §12 checklist on finished drafts.
- **Resolve every `[VERIFY]`** (brands, tasting notes, winery/bar names, ABV, prices, scores,
  pairings) via live research. If unconfirmable, cut the claim or hold the row. Never publish an
  unverified specific.

## Step 6 — Hero expansion
Expand each Hero from its v1 skeleton to full Word Target (~1,600–1,800w/lang) with sourcing and
the repurpose tree (§13). Schedule as its own work block.

## Step 7 — Date re-flow & calendar QA
Re-flow Publish Dates: even 2–3/day, 1 WN + 1 LIQ9 where possible, timely pinned, weekends lighter,
no two similar adjacent. Build the filtered views (WN / LIQ9 / All-by-week).

## Step 8 — Handoff & Definition of Done
Internal hub-spoke links wired (Final URL), schema + images attached, repurpose tree logged,
Status → Done → scheduled → Published.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Parameters locked (volume / data / focus)
- [ ] Live signals pulled; sales-ban dates confirmed & pinned
- [ ] 6–8 clusters; hub-spoke mapped; ~50/50 brand
- [ ] Council pre-flight on the plan + owner sign-off
- [ ] New month DB (schema mirrored; Month option added)
- [ ] Rows built: metadata + brief + bilingual v1 at Review
- [ ] Compliance applied (sale-naming, holy-day, no-emoji)
- [ ] Council QA; every [VERIFY] resolved
- [ ] Heroes expanded + repurpose trees
- [ ] Dates re-flowed; team views built
- [ ] Handoff (links, schema, images, Final URL)
