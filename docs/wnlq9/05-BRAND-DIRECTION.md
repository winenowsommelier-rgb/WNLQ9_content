# 05 — Brand Direction (Wine-Now & LIQ9)

> Mirror of §16–17 of the Notion Standard. The deep style guide each desk writes from.
> Universal test: every piece should read like advice from a brilliant, knowledgeable friend —
> not a salesperson, professor, or algorithm. TH + EN are two native voices, never translations.

## Wine-Now (WN)
- **Positioning:** Bangkok's sommelier, in your pocket. **Premium = quality + fit, never status.**
- **Audience:** Thai urban professionals 28–50 getting into wine (TH-first); expats/connoisseurs (EN); gift buyers.
- **Voice:** Thai-first magazine warmth; calm authority; never academic, never breathless.
  - No hyperbole — be sensory-specific ("Albariño for a 35-degree Bangkok afternoon," not "a great wine").
  - Never "drink this" — always *why this bottle, this moment, this table.*
  - We say no, we explain (story not spec sheet), we remember the reader (LINE OA), we're Thai.
- **Lexicon — lean in:** pairing, structure, acidity, body, occasion, value, progression, serving temperature.
  **Avoid:** "best ever," "amazing," "deal," "cheap," "must-buy," status language, hard sell.
- **Categories owned:** Education · Pairing (Thai food first) · Spotlights (producers, Thai/New-Latitude wine) · Travel (Khao Yai, BKK wine bars) · Tips.
- **CTA:** "Ask the Wine-Now sommelier desk on LINE" · price-on-request · responsible drinking, 20+.
- **Is NOT:** a supermarket, a wine school, or "premium for status."

## LIQ9
- **Positioning:** the spirits intelligence behind Bangkok's bar scene.
- **Audience:** cocktail-curious 25–45; home bartenders; the bar/hospitality crowd; gifting.
- **Voice:** Master-of-Malt wit + Bangkok-bartender confidence. Short-sentence punch; em-dash rhythm;
  insider but never gatekeeping; earn the exclamation mark.
  - Decode, don't lecture — context (the cocktail, the culture) before the bottle/spec.
  - Cocktail-culture lens always present — even a sipping whisky gets a highball note.
  - Name real Bangkok bars/bartenders (e.g. Bar Us, Dry Wave, BKK Social Club, Vesper).
- **Lexicon — lean in:** build, stir/shake, neat, on the rocks, highball, agave, dram, garnish.
  **Avoid:** bro-y excess, fake scarcity, lecture tone, jargon with no payoff.
- **Categories owned:** Education (spirit 101s, technique, tools) · Recipes (Recipe schema) ·
  Spotlights (Thai hero spirits) · Travel (BKK cocktail bars) · Tips.
- **Thai hero spirits (verified):** Iron Balls Gin (Bangkok — lemongrass, ginger, coconut-pineapple
  base) · Chalong Bay rum (Phuket — agricole, 100% first-press cane juice). Re-confirm notes before publish.
- **CTA:** "Ask the LIQ9 bartender desk on LINE" · price-on-request · drink mindfully, 20+.
- **Is NOT:** a Wishbeer list, a duty-free, an importer, or whisky-only.

## E-E-A-T (both)
A real, named, credentialed human stands behind every byline (WSET/sommelier for WN; bartender/
industry for LIQ9). Author bio + photo + credentials + `Person` schema on every published page.
Do not invent personas. First-person experience ("we tasted/tested") is the strongest signal.
