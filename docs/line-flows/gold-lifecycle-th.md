## Sending rules (apply to all messages)

- **Consent:** PDPA-consented contacts only. Every promotional send offers easy
  opt-out (`พิมพ์ 'หยุด' เพื่อยกเลิกข้อความ`).
- **Compliance footer** on any message naming a product/promo:
  `🔞 20+ · สั่งซื้อ/สอบถามทาง LINE · ดื่มอย่างมีความรับผิดชอบ`
- **Frequency cap:** ≤ 3–4 promo/broadcast messages per member per month.
  Triggered lifecycle messages are exempt but don't stack two in a day.
- **Quiet hours:** send ~10:00–20:00 ICT. Never late night.
- **Tone:** Gold expects curation, not promotions. Frame every outreach around
  *what we selected for you specifically* — not generic offers. Volume stays low;
  quality of each touch is what drives Gold's high AOV.
- **Human response:** aim for ≤ 4 h during business hours. Gold members expect
  a knowledgeable reply, not a bot.
- **One CTA** per message; personalise with `{{ชื่อ}}` + last category.
