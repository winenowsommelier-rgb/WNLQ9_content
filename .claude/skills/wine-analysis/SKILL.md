## Wine Content Analysis Framework

### Sommelier Credibility Assessment

Evaluate expertise indicators:
- **Certifications**: WSET, Master Sommelier, Certified Sommelier credentials
- **Experience**: Years in industry, wine regions visited, formal training
- **Knowledge Depth**: Wine history, production methods, terroir understanding
- **Communication**: Clarity of explanations, avoiding pretension, accessibility

### Tasting Notes Quality

Validate tasting notes for:
- **Accuracy**: Common descriptors for the wine type and vintage
- **Clarity**: Clear progression from appearance to nose to palate
- **Credibility**: Specific observations grounded in wine science
- **Accessibility**: Avoiding gatekeeping language, explaining technical terms

### Wine Education Content

Assess educational content on:
- **Accuracy**: Factual correctness about wine science and history
- **Completeness**: Covers key learning objectives for the topic
- **Audience Fit**: Appropriate level for target audience (beginner/intermediate/expert)
- **Engagement**: Uses examples, stories, and multi-sensory language

### Wine Pairing Analysis

Evaluate pairing guides for:
- **Wine Selection**: Appropriate wine characteristics for the food
- **Rationale**: Clear explanation of flavor/texture matching principles
- **Alternatives**: Offering flexibility in price point and availability
- **Cultural Context**: Respecting traditional and innovative pairings

### Wine Region Expertise

Validate region knowledge:
- **Terroir Understanding**: Climate, soil, altitude factors
- **Signature Wines**: Key grapes, producers, and styles
- **Production Methods**: Winemaking traditions specific to region
- **Current Trends**: Modern movements and sustainability practices

## Wine SEO Strategy

### Wine-Specific Keywords

Target keywords by type:
- **Varietal keywords**: "Pinot Noir", "Chardonnay", "Cabernet Sauvignon"
- **Region keywords**: "Napa Valley wines", "Bordeaux region", "Rioja wines"
- **Style keywords**: "Natural wines", "Organic wines", "Orange wines"
- **Pairing keywords**: "Wine pairing guide", "White wine with chicken"
- **Educational keywords**: "How to taste wine", "Wine tasting notes"

### Wine Schema Markup

Recommend structured data:
- **Product Schema**: For wine products with tasting notes, region, varietal
- **Organization Schema**: Sommelier credentials and expertise
- **Review Schema**: Wine reviews and ratings with expert credentials
- **LocalBusiness Schema**: For wine bars, vineyards, tasting rooms

### Wine E-Commerce SEO

Optimize for:
- **Product Pages**: Varietal, region, vintage, price, availability
- **Inventory Management**: Stock status, allocation updates
- **Shipping Compliance**: Wine shipping laws by region
- **Marketplace Optimization**: Integration with Vivino, Wine-Searcher, etc.

## Quality Gates

Wine content standards:

| Content Type | Minimum Requirement |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Tasting Notes | At least 3 dimensions (appearance, nose, palate) |
| Wine Pairing | Wine selection rationale + at least 1 alternative |
| Region Guide | Terroir context + 3+ signature wines |
| Sommelier Bio | At least 1 verifiable credential |
| Wine Education | 500+ words with clear learning objectives |
| Product Page | Varietal + Region + Vintage + Tasting Notes |

## Sommelier Credibility Scoring

**Credibility Score (0-100)**: Weighted assessment

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Verifiable Certifications | 30% |
| Industry Experience | 25% |
| Knowledge Depth & Accuracy | 25% |
| Communication Quality | 20% |

### Certification Tiers

- **Master Sommelier**: Highest credential (requires ~2000 hours study)
- **WSET Level 3 or Higher**: Advanced wine knowledge
- **Certified Sommelier**: Wine service and knowledge baseline
- **Sommelier in Training**: Entry-level qualification
