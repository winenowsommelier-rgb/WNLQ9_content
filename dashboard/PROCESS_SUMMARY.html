Setup Steps (Detailed)

  1. Desktop: Click your workspace name (top left) → "Settings & Administration" → "Manage apps"
  2. Web: Go to https://YOUR-WORKSPACE.slack.com/apps/manage
  3. Search for "Incoming Webhooks" (official Slack app)
  4. Click "Install" or "Add to Slack"
  5. Confirm permissions if prompted
  6. Click "Add New Webhook to Workspace"
  7. Select channel: #content-workflow (create if needed)
  8. Click "Create New Webhook"
  9. Copy the entire webhook URL (starts with https://hooks.slack.com/services/...)
⚠️ If You're Stuck: See SLACK_WEBHOOK_SETUP.md in the dashboard folder for detailed troubleshooting and screenshots.
